AIMS · January 1, 2027

January 1, 2027 — the one platform you're forced to buy.

Ontario's Assurance and Improvement in Medication Safety (AIMS) program makes a compliant incident-reporting platform mandatory. If you have to buy one anyway, it should earn its keep.

Who it binds

Every accredited Ontario pharmacy — roughly 4,500 of them — on the same day. No phase-in, no exemptions.

What it requires

OCP's published Platform Criteria: 15 mandatory incident fields and a daily anonymous upload to the national database (NIDR).

What it pays

Nothing on its own. The real question is what else the platform you're required to run can do for your pharmacy.
